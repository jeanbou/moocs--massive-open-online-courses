## Course Overview

This course provides a **practical framework for transforming an entrepreneurial idea into a real venture**.

It guides you through the key steps required to move from a simple idea to a structured and validated business opportunity.

You will learn how to:

- Identify and characterize a market  
- Understand customer needs  
- Build and test a prototype (MVP)  
- Define a business model  
- Refine your strategic vision  

The course focuses on real-world entrepreneurial execution rather than theory alone.

---

## Objectives of the Course

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Analyze and define a target market  
- Evaluate customer needs and expectations  
- Design and test a minimum viable product (MVP)  
- Understand and build a business model  
- Adapt your idea based on feedback and iteration  
- Develop a structured entrepreneurial project  

---

## Course Structure

### 1. Characterize your market
- Define your target market  
- Understand customer segments  
- Analyze market opportunities  
- Identify value propositions  

### 2. Test and prototype your idea
- Move from idea to product  
- Build a minimum viable product (MVP)  
- Understand iteration and pivoting  
- Validate assumptions with real users  

### 3. Use the business model as a guide
- Understand the business model framework  
- Structure revenue and value creation  
- Apply the model to real cases  
- Connect execution with strategy  

### 4. Strategic thinking
- Understand strategic implications of decisions  
- Analyze system dynamics in a business  
- Identify risks and growth opportunities  
- Refine long-term vision
