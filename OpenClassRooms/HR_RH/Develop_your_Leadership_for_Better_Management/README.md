## Course Overview

This course helps you develop essential **leadership skills** to become a more effective manager in a professional environment.

Leadership is not just about authority - it is about influence, communication, and the ability to guide a team toward success.

In this course, you will learn how to:

- Adopt a leadership mindset in your workplace  
- Manage and motivate team members effectively  
- Set objectives and evaluate performance  
- Provide constructive feedback  
- Handle mistakes and conflicts  
- Improve communication and decision-making within teams
