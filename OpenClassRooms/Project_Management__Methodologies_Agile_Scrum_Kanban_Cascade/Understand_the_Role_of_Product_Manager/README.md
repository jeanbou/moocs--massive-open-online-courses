## Course Overview

This course introduces the **role of a Product Manager (PM)** and explains how product management works in modern digital organizations.

A Product Manager is responsible for defining, building, and improving a product that delivers value to users and meets business goals.

They act as a bridge between:

- Business strategy  
- Technical teams  
- User experience and design  

The course explains how Product Managers coordinate teams and drive product success from idea to delivery.

---

## Key Responsibilities of a Product Manager

By the end of this course, you will understand that a Product Manager typically:

- Defines product vision and strategy  
- Understands user needs through research and data  
- Prioritizes features and manages the product backlog  
- Collaborates with engineers, designers, and stakeholders  
- Writes user stories and defines requirements  
- Tracks product performance using KPIs  
- Iterates based on feedback and market changes
