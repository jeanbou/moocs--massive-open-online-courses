## Module Knowledge Check Answers

1. What activity happens during the pre-processing stage of speech recognition?

Correct answer:
Feature vectors are extracted from the audio waveform for modeling.

---

2. What are phonemes?

Correct answer:
The smallest unit of sound in speech.

---

3. Why is it important to generate prosody in speech synthesis?

Correct answer:
Prosody ensures natural pronunciation and speech cadence.
